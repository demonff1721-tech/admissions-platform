# Alliance University Admissions, Eligibility & Fee Structures

- **MBA Eligibility:** Bachelor's degree in any discipline with min 50% aggregate marks (45% SC/ST). Accepted tests: CAT, MAT, XAT, NMAT, CMAT, GMAT, AMAT.
- **MBA Tuition Fee:** INR 15,00,000 (Fifteen Lakhs) payable in four semester installments. Registration: INR 50,000.
- **Hostel Fees:** INR 1,20,000 to INR 1,80,000 per year depending on occupancy.
- **B.Tech Eligibility:** 10+2 with Physics & Mathematics along with Chemistry/CS/Biotechnology with min 50% aggregate marks. Accepted tests: JEE Main, COMED-K, KCET, AUSAT.
- **Law (BBA LLB) Eligibility:** 10+2 with min 45% aggregate marks (40% SC/ST). Accepted tests: CLAT, LSAT India, AUSAT.
